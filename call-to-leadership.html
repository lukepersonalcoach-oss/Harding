The course

Leadership isn't one thing

The Call to Leadership is grounded in Integral Theory — a metatheory of psychological development — offering a way of understanding the different mindsets, or "worldviews", that shape how people lead: some built around structure and order, some around results and achievement, some around collaboration and inclusion, some around adaptive, systemic thinking.

Most leaders default to one or two of these. The most effective leaders can move between them depending on what the moment calls for. The session helps participants recognise their own default, understand its blind spots, and build the range to lead differently when the situation demands it.

Format

Not a slide deck

Rather than a lecture on leadership theory, the session is built around challenging games, collaboration and structured reflection — participants work through their own values and current challenges rather than sitting through abstractions. It deliberately challenges participants, pushing past comfortable answers to something more honest. It's designed to be immersive and a little different from the usual corporate training format, while staying grounded in a research-based, well-established model of adult development.
